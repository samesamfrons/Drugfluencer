About The Project

The Drugfluencer is a multi-format narrative project that tells the story of Zoe Winters, a drug-positive influencer whose "authentic" content about harm reduction slowly becomes the content that kills her. The project explores themes of algorithmic exploitation, creator culture, distributed complicity, and the attention economy through multiple narrative formats: feature screenplay, novellas, short films, interactive choose-your-own-adventure, and visual storyboards.

Core Concept: A four-act documentary-within-a-documentary about distributed complicity. Flux Technologies developed "Project Cassandra"—a predictive analytics program that forecasts creator mortality and optimizes it for revenue. The genius of the system: no single person commits murder. Everyone just follows incentives. The death emerges from the optimization itself.

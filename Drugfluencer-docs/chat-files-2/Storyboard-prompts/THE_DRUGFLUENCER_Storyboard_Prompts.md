# THE DRUGFLUENCER
## Storyboard Image Generation Prompts

---

## VISUAL STYLE NOTES

**Overall Aesthetic:** The film oscillates between two visual languages:
1. **Documentary/Interview segments** - Clinical, white backdrop, ring light glow, sterile and accusatory
2. **Flashback sequences** - Desaturated glamour, handheld intimacy, European art-film melancholy mixed with Safdie Brothers' sweaty close-ups

**Color Palette:**
- Present day: Cold whites, institutional grays, legal-document beige
- Berlin era: Wet cobblestones, amber streetlights, club blacks and neons
- London era: Steel and glass, designer minimalism, money-green accents
- LA era: Overexposed sunlight, pharmaceutical whites, swimming pool blues
- NYC era: 3AM bodega fluorescents, lonely apartment warmth, terminal isolation

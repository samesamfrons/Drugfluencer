### Cinematic Style: Lynch × Safdie Brothers × Wong Kar-wai

## MASTER STYLE DIRECTIVE

**Apply to ALL prompts:**

"Cinematic still in the hybrid style of David Lynch's uncanny Americana meets Safdie Brothers' anxious handheld intimacy meets Wong Kar-wai's romantic melancholy. Shot on 35mm film with visible grain, anamorphic lens distortion, and chromatic aberration. Color grading oscillates between Lynch's saturated suburban surrealism (deep reds, electric blues, sodium vapor yellows), Safdie Brothers' sweaty fluorescent naturalism (greenish whites, bodega glow, NYC grime), and Wong Kar-wai/Christopher Doyle's smeared neon romanticism (step-printed motion blur, expired film stocks, rain-streaked colors bleeding). Shallow depth of field with faces sharp against blown-out backgrounds. The uncanny domesticity of Blue Velvet, the claustrophobic urgency of Uncut Gems, the yearning slow-motion of In the Mood for Love. Contemporary subject matter shot like a fever dream remembered through tears."

**Technical specifications:**
- 35mm film grain, Kodak Vision3 500T or Fuji Eterna stocks
- Anamorphic 2.39:1 aspect ratio with oval bokeh
- Practical lighting: neon, fluorescent, sodium vapor, phone screens
- Shallow depth of field, faces emerging from blur
- Step-printing and motion blur for emotional moments
- Chromatic aberration on highlights
- Lens flares from practical sources
- Handheld micro-movements even in "still" compositions
- Color temperature mixing (warm/cool in same frame)

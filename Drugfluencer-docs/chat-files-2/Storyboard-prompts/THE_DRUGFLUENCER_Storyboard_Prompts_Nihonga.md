## MASTER STYLE GUIDE

**All prompts should be prefixed with this style directive:**

> "In the style of contemporary Japanese Nihonga painting, using mineral pigments on silk, with visible brushwork and gold leaf accents, combining traditional Japanese artistic techniques with modern subject matter, flat perspective with atmospheric depth, muted earth tones punctuated by luminous color, the aesthetic of Hiroshi Senju meets Makoto Fujimura, delicate linework with areas of bold pigment, wabi-sabi imperfection, contemporary ukiyo-e sensibility depicting the floating world of social media—"

**Key Nihonga Elements to Include:**
- Mineral pigment texture (iwaenogu)
- Gold and silver leaf accents (kinpaku/ginpaku)
- Silk or washi paper texture visible
- Flat compositional space with atmospheric perspective
- Negative space (ma) as compositional element
- Seasonal/natural elements integrated symbolically
- Delicate linework (sumi ink outlines)
- Layered translucent washes
- Cloud/mist motifs (kasumi/kumo)
- Contemporary subjects rendered with classical techniques

**Color Palette (Nihonga Traditional):**
- Gofun (oyster shell white)
- Shu (vermillion)
- Ai (indigo)
- Moegi (yellow-green)
- Rokusho (malachite green)
- Shinju (pearl)
- Kin (gold)
- Gin (silver)

## USAGE NOTES

For consistent results across all images, begin each prompt with the master style prefix:

**"In the style of contemporary Japanese Nihonga painting, using mineral pigments on silk, with visible brushwork and gold leaf accents, combining traditional Japanese artistic techniques with modern subject matter—"**

Then append the specific scene description.

**Recommended reference artists for style guidance:**
- Hiroshi Senju (contemporary waterfalls and cliffs)
- Makoto Fujimura (abstract Nihonga with gold leaf)
- Rieko Morita (contemporary figurative)
- Matazo Kayama (modern traditional synthesis)
- Atsushi Uemura (bijin-ga influence)

**Technical notes for image generation:**
- Emphasize mineral pigment texture and visible brushwork
- Include gold/silver leaf accents in lighting effects
- Use traditional color names (gofun, shu, ai) in prompts
- Reference silk or washi paper texture
- Include atmospheric perspective and mist (kasumi)
- Embrace negative space (ma) in composition
- Add seasonal elements symbolically where appropriate

# THE DRUGFLUENCER
## Short Film: "47 SECONDS"
### Runtime: 4:47

---

## CONCEPT

A vertical-friendly short that hooks in the first 3 seconds, delivers information in rapid bursts, uses text overlays like native TikTok content, and ends on a cliffhanger that demands the feature. The entire short is structured around ONE damning detail: the 47 seconds Alex watched before calling 911.

**Format:** Can be shot/released in both 9:16 (vertical/TikTok/Reels) and 16:9 (horizontal/YouTube/theatrical)

**Hook Strategy:** Open with the most disturbing image, then rewind to explain it.

# TIKTOK OPTIMIZATION NOTES

## Hook Strategy (First 3 Seconds)
- Open on the most disturbing image (unconscious woman on livestream)
- Text overlay delivers the hook immediately ("She watched for 47 seconds")
- No title card until after the hook lands

## Retention Tactics
- **Text overlays** throughout—TikTok native behavior
- **No shot longer than 15 seconds** except the climax
- **Pattern interrupts** every 10-15 seconds (new location, new character, new revelation)
- **Sound design** as hook—notification sounds trigger dopamine response
- **Cliffhanger structure**—each act ends with a question

## Vertical Optimization
- All shots can be reframed 9:16
- Text overlays positioned for vertical
- Close-ups preferred (faces fill vertical frame better)
- Interview footage works both ways

## Series Potential
This short sets up:
- **"47 SECONDS: THE BOYFRIEND"** - Marcus's perspective
- **"47 SECONDS: THE DEALER"** - Jay's perspective  
- **"47 SECONDS: THE SISTER"** - Rebecca's investigation
- **"47 SECONDS: ZOE"** - Her POV (the prequel)

Each short is 3-5 minutes, ends on cliffhanger, drives to feature.

# MUSIC/SOUND DESIGN NOTES

## Approach
Minimal score. Let silence and diegetic sound carry the tension. When music appears, it should feel wrong—too pleasant, too corporate, too TikTok.

## Key Sound Elements
- **Notification sounds** - Used as percussion throughout
- **Heartbeat** - Underlies tense moments
- **Flatline** - The death
- **Silence** - The most powerful tool

## No Licensed Music
All sound should be original/licensable to avoid TikTok muting and to control tone completely.

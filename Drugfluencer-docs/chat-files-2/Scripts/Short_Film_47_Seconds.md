# THE DRUGFLUENCER
## Short Film: "47 SECONDS"
### Runtime: 4:47 | Vertical Format (9:16) | TikTok/Reels Optimized

---

## CONCEPT

A cold open hook that drops you into the climax: the 47 seconds Alex watched before calling 911. Then we pull back to show how we got here—but never fully explain. The short is designed to create obsessive curiosity: Who is she? Why didn't they call? What's Project Cassandra?

**Structure:** Hook (0:00-0:15) → Rewind (0:15-3:30) → Return to the moment (3:30-4:30) → Sting (4:30-4:47)

## TECHNICAL NOTES

### Format Specifications
- **Aspect Ratio:** 9:16 (vertical/TikTok native)
- **Alt Version:** 1:1 (Instagram feed)
- **Runtime:** 4:47 (under 5 min limit)
- **Captions:** Burned-in, stylized, essential for sound-off viewing

### Pacing Strategy
- **0:00-0:03:** Hook must land immediately—crisis visible in first frame
- **Cuts average 3-7 seconds** in first half (attention economy)
- **Cuts extend to 8-12 seconds** in back half (earned attention)
- **The 47 seconds sequence** plays closer to real-time—the discomfort is intentional

### Sound Design
- Notification sounds as leitmotif throughout
- Silence used strategically (the 47 seconds)
- No score until end—the content is the content

### Visual Hierarchy
- Interview space = stark white, clinical, interrogation
- Flashbacks = stylized (Lynch/WKW/Safdie depending on era)
- Stream footage = degraded, screen-recorded, uncomfortably authentic
- Documents = flat, bureaucratic, horror in plainness

### Caption/Text Strategy
- Names and roles appear on first interview appearance
- Timeline stamps ground the viewer
- Final statistics land without commentary
- "COMING 2025" / "Follow for more" = clear CTA
